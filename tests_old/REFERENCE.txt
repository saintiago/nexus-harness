Archived test reference. These files are excluded from active validation.
Their original imports and paths are intentionally preserved; they are not a runnable suite here.
They are reference material, not an alternate gate, and none of it should be copied back
wholesale; docs/testing.md is the contract the active layers follow.

The active suites live in the directory of the layer that owns their behavior. Each archived file
below is kept for the behavior named beside the active suite that now carries it:

- agent.test.ts — the coding-runtime adapter: the launch every turn is given, the prefix a
  diagnostic launch refuses, the prompt path into a real runtime, the completion streams that are
  not a turn and stopping the runtime a turn started (tests/unit/codex-launch.test.ts,
  tests/boundary/agent-runtime.test.ts), and the activity lines its stream produces
  (tests/unit/runtime-events.test.ts).
- checks.test.ts — what one configured command does with its output, its limit and a caller's stop
  (tests/boundary/process.test.ts), the run timeline (tests/boundary/report.test.ts), and the
  setup/check round's order and endings, from setup-then-checks to a red round kept for repair
  (tests/boundary/check-round.test.ts).
- jira.test.ts — the gateway route, credential and refusal classification
  (tests/boundary/jira-gateway.test.ts); the configured queue's search, the map onto the four-field
  task, the claim that refuses an issue whose revision or status changed, the workspace pointer,
  the result publication and the ticket's own conversation
  (tests/boundary/jira-connector.test.ts); the source configuration's defaults
  (tests/unit/config.test.ts).
- queue-recovery.test.ts — authoritative queue recovery before a fresh claim: the In Review
  resume, the refusal of a still-running or ambiguously owned ticket, and the fresh read that
  decides Done and a retained repair (tests/boundary/jira-connector.test.ts).
- refresh.test.ts — source readiness between two queue tickets
  (tests/boundary/source-readiness.test.ts).
- review-github.test.ts — the review scan's GitHub reads (tests/boundary/github-review.test.ts).
- reviews.test.ts — the scan's evidence and decisions (tests/unit/reviews.test.ts), assembled in
  tests/workflow/review-handoff.test.ts.
- completion-github.test.ts, completion-arm.test.ts — the completion path's own GitHub commands:
  every read with the reader credential, the one auto-merge mutation with the operator's, and the
  refusal diagnostics (tests/boundary/completion-commands.test.ts); the gate, merge and Jira
  decisions (tests/unit/completion.test.ts), assembled in
  tests/workflow/delivery-completion.test.ts.
- completion-cli.test.ts — the completion pass's run and the counts it reports
  (tests/unit/completion.test.ts), assembled in tests/workflow/delivery-completion.test.ts.
- report.test.ts — the final report, the timeline beside it, and what writing one leaves alone
  (tests/boundary/report.test.ts), assembled in tests/workflow/execution-repair.test.ts.
- cli.test.ts — the command line's help, refusals and exit codes, and the configuration command
  that creates and starts nothing (tests/unit/cli.test.ts).
- runner-records.test.ts, runner-repair.test.ts, runner.test.ts — the run's bounded repair loop,
  its stop and timeout endings, its ledger record and its conversation history
  (tests/unit/runner.test.ts, tests/unit/escalation.test.ts, tests/unit/history.test.ts,
  tests/boundary/workspace-continuation.test.ts, tests/boundary/report.test.ts), assembled in
  tests/workflow/execution-repair.test.ts and tests/workflow/continuation.test.ts.
- history.test.ts, history-source.test.ts — the conversation snapshot, its store and the
  developer-report publication policy (tests/unit/history.test.ts).
- queue.test.ts — the serial queue's decisions, its phases, its watch mode and its recovery
  (tests/unit/queue.test.ts), with intake continuation in tests/workflow/continuation.test.ts.
- baseline.test.ts, baseline-queue.integration.test.ts — the baseline findings, the guidance they
  produce and the outcomes they decide (tests/unit/escalation.test.ts), with the retained
  workspace the next attempt continues in tests/workflow/continuation.test.ts.
- cli-run.integration.test.ts, local-run.integration.test.ts — a run through the built command,
  its clone, its checks, its repair and its report (tests/workflow/execution-repair.test.ts), on
  top of the real process, Git and filesystem contracts (tests/boundary/process.test.ts,
  tests/boundary/git.test.ts, tests/boundary/workspace.test.ts).
- source-cli.integration.test.ts, queue-cli.test.ts, lifecycle.test.ts — the source and queue
  consumer paths (tests/unit/queue.test.ts, tests/workflow/continuation.test.ts) and the Jira
  claim, pointer and publication contracts (tests/boundary/jira-connector.test.ts).

What no active suite reaches yet, and what stays here as its reference: the terminal presentation
the activity pane draws (activity.test.ts), the source coordinator's receipts, claims and
publication around one run (source.test.ts), the pre-delivery baseline diagnosis pass
(baseline.test.ts), and the review watch's own timing (reviews.test.ts). The decisions those
passes are built from are carried by the suites named above.

The live provider exercises (live-verifier.test.ts, live/) require explicit authorization and stay
outside the ordinary gate (docs/validation-caching.md). The fixture modules these files share
(fixtures/*.ts, support.ts, reviews-shared.ts) stay beside them as reference.
