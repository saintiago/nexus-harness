Archived test reference. These files are excluded from active validation.
Their original imports and paths are intentionally preserved; they are not a runnable suite here.
Rebuild required behavior coverage in tests/ using docs/testing.md.
Suites whose coverage the rebuilt layers now carry were removed as that coverage was
rebuilt; what remains is the coverage that still belongs to another layer.

The active suites live in the directory of the layer that owns their behavior. The rebuilt unit
layer (tests/unit/) replaced config.test.ts, completion-config.test.ts, completion-gate.test.ts,
completion-policy.test.ts, completion-summary.test.ts, runner-policy.test.ts, history-runner.test.ts
and baseline-findings.test.ts. The rebuilt boundary layer (tests/boundary/) replaced git.test.ts,
workspace.test.ts, workspace-branch.test.ts, stop.test.ts and delivery.test.ts. The rebuilt
workflow layer (tests/workflow/) carries the assembled connections those suites below exercised
only through whole-workflow fixtures: a task that runs and is repaired in the same working copy
(execution-repair.test.ts), a delivered attempt handed to the reviewer through a pinned view of
the workspace its pointer names (review-handoff.test.ts), a delivery finished only on the merge
GitHub made at the revision it published (delivery-completion.test.ts), and an interrupted
attempt continued, in the same clone, by the next attempt (continuation.test.ts).

What stays here is the coverage those rebuilds did not carry, grouped by the layer that still owes
it: the coding-runtime adapter (agent.test.ts), the configured setup/check round (checks.test.ts),
the Jira connector's queue, claim, pointer and publication behavior (jira.test.ts,
queue-recovery.test.ts, source.test.ts), source readiness (refresh.test.ts), the review scan's
GitHub reads (review-github.test.ts) and its remaining decisions (reviews.test.ts), the completion
path's GitHub commands (completion-github.test.ts, completion-arm.test.ts, completion-cli.test.ts),
reporting and terminal presentation (report.test.ts, activity.test.ts, cli.test.ts), the file-task
command surface (cli.integration.test.ts, cli-run.integration.test.ts, local-run.integration.test.ts),
the source and queue consumer paths with their pre-delivery diagnosis (source-cli.integration.test.ts,
baseline.test.ts, baseline-queue.integration.test.ts, queue-cli.test.ts, queue.test.ts,
lifecycle.test.ts), the records and repair history (runner-records.test.ts, runner-repair.test.ts,
runner.test.ts, history.test.ts, history-source.test.ts), and the live provider exercises
(live-verifier.test.ts, live/). The fixture modules these files share (fixtures/*.ts, support.ts,
reviews-shared.ts) stay with them as reference; none of it is an alternate gate, and none of it
should be copied back wholesale.
